About Lesotho

The Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is a small, beautiful country located in Southern Africa and completely surrounded by South Africa.

It is known as the Mountain Kingdom because of its high mountains and beautiful natural environment.

The country is famous for its Basotho culture, traditional clothing, music, food and hospitality.

Basotho Culture

Traditional Clothing

The Basotho blanket is an important part of traditional Basotho clothing and culture.

Traditional Food

Lesotho has traditional foods such as papa, moroho and other local dishes.

Hospitality

Basotho people are known for welcoming visitors and sharing their culture.

Things to Experience in Lesotho

  • Mountain hiking and sightseeing.
  • Horse riding in the mountains.
  • Visiting traditional villages.
  • Exploring waterfalls.
  • Learning about Basotho culture.
